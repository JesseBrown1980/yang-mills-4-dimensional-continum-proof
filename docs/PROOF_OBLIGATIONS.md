## Irreducible distinctions

- The finite-lattice SU(2) Wilson action is **not** the continuum existence theorem.
- A finite S₃ Hamiltonian result in 2D space is **not** a four-dimensional continuous SU(2) result.
- The finite subgroups Q8, 2T, 2O, 2I do not, simply by being studied together, form a limit that fills continuous SU(2).
- Four-dimensional Euclidean lattice gauge invariance is a baseline test. It is not evidence of a physical continuum gap.
- If a positive lattice gap is found, the bound in physical units and its behavior under a→0 still must be proved.

# YM4-VOL01 — local magnetic first-variation comparison at spatial sizes L=2,3,4,5

**2026-11-22. Evidence class: exact integer support combinatorics, awaiting hosted replay.** This is the first larger-spatial-volume probe after WG09's fixed-L=2 small-coupling first-neutral Wilson ordering theorem. It is an obstruction and design input, not a volume-uniform or continuum theorem.

## Model identity

Use the original continuous SU(2) Wilson transfer, periodic spatial lattice \((\mathbf Z/L\mathbf Z)^3\), one independent positively oriented link per site/axis, all three spatial plaquette orientations and fundamental trace character basis. For each elementary plaquette \(p\), let \(\phi_p=\chi_{1/2}(U_p)\) and \(W_p=\phi_p/2\). Let \(V=\sum_p W_p\).

For distinct normalized plaquette spin networks \(\phi_p,\phi_q\), any nonzero linkwise Haar integral \(\langle\phi_p,W_r\phi_q\rangle\) requires **even fundamental multiplicity on every link**. Thus its supports must satisfy \(m_p\oplus m_q=m_r\), where \(m\) denotes the set of links with nontrivial fundamental occupation. This is a *necessary* filter, sufficient here to show vanishing.

## Exact geometry and combinatorial results

For **every \(L\ge3\)**, distinct elementary plaquettes share **at most one oriented link**. Hence their symmetric difference occupies 6 or 8 links, whereas any elementary plaquette occupies exactly four. Therefore \(m_p\oplus m_q\ne m_r\). Diagonal \(\langle\phi_p,W_r\phi_p\rangle\) also vanishes because the single plaquette insertion has odd link occupancy. Consequently,

\[
P_{\mathrm{plaq}}VP_{\mathrm{plaq}}=0
\qquad\text{on the fundamental single-plaquette spin-network span for }L\ge3.
\]

The numerical row enumeration is a falsifier of the geometric lemma, not a stand-in for its all-L proof.

The WG06 L=2 positive first magnetic variation is generated by the **additional 18 pairs of two-link winding loops**, creating six \(K_{4,2}\) blocks joining plaquette and double-winding support. When L>=3, an elementary periodic winding has length L, so two disjoint windings have at least 2L>4 fundamental occupied links. The exceptional L=2 rank-42 magnetic graph and its six positive slopes consequently **cannot be carried unchanged** into larger-volume four-fundamental electric bands.

For L>=3, a neutral gauge singlet with exactly four fundamental occupied links and no higher-spin links must form a length-four closed graph cycle. For L>=3, these are elementary contractible plaquettes or (only when L=4) axial length-four winding cycles. A single axial winding carries nontrivial global centre flux; therefore the **neutral four-fundamental basis consists of the 3L³ elementary plaquette states**. The L=3 axial length-three windings are also globally centre-charged and occur at a distinct electric order. This is a graph and sector statement, not yet a spectral ordering theorem.

## Where the next proof must move

At L>=3, the first neutral magnetic splitting of the fundamental plaquette band starts **no earlier than second order** in the magnetic multiplier, and it may interact with other physical electric modes through the original vacuum and the all-spin resolvent. The WG09 six-mode third-order symmetry algebra belongs to the exceptional L=2 winding topology.

The next verifiable mathematical object is the centred second-order local plaquette kernel

\[
H_{2,L}=P_{\rm plaq}\big(\tfrac12V^2-VP_{\rm vac}V\big)P_{\rm plaq}
+\text{certified higher-spin Schur term},
\]

with each matrix element organized by overlapping plaquette clusters and a **volume-independent bound on the connected remainder**. Direct global norm estimates \(\|V\|\le3L^3\) deteriorate with volume and should be replaced by connected/cumulant or local polymer bounds.

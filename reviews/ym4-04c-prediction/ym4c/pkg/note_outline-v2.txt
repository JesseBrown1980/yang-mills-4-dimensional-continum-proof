- Why a capped fixed-point iteration is not used. The pinned planted variant (alpha_0 = 0, alpha_{j+1} = S^+, MET at the first j with s_j <= alpha_j(1 + 10^-40), 400 evaluations) reads the crude edge at 0.01901/0.01902 instead of 0.01904/0.01905. Two other variants behave differently: inflation by 10001/10000 keeps MET through 0.01904, and acceptance without tolerance loses it from 0.01901.

- The pinned grids: theta up to 21/4, beta on 10^-5, delta_crit^- on 10^-9, and 160-bit upward rounding.

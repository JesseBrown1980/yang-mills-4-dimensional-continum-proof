## 2. VOL14 risk: straight-link blocking leaves weak coupling after about log β steps

Under straight-link blocking, a coarse plaquette **is** a fine b×b Wilson loop. In 4D, a Wilson loop carries a perimeter (self-energy) term. At leading order in 1/β (Feynman gauge, lattice Green's function on a 96⁴-mode torus), I computed

$$\langle 1-\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}W_{b\times b}\rangle=\frac{3}{2\beta}S(b),\qquad S(1)=\tfrac12\ \text{(reproduces the known }3/(4\beta)\text{)},$$

| b | S(b) | S(b)/b | coarse β_eff/β = 1/(2S(b)) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.500 | 0.50 | 1.000 |
| 2 | 1.369 | 0.68 | 0.365 |
| 3 | 2.305 | 0.77 | 0.217 |
| 9 | 8.155 | 0.91 | 0.061 |
| 27 | 26.13 | 0.97 | 0.019 |

S(b) grows **linearly** in b (the perimeter law). So each ternary straight-link step **divides** the effective plaquette coupling by about 4.6. Asymptotic freedom, by contrast, only **subtracts** about 0.41 from β per ternary step (SU(2), one loop).

At β = 9⁵, the decimated theory's plaquettes look O(1)-coupled after about **8 ternary steps** (around 6.6×10³ lattice spacings). The physical length is about exp(3π²β/11) ≈ **10^69028** lattice spacings.

**Consequence.** The straight-link coarse theory becomes strongly fluctuating at the plaquette level purely from UV self-energy, while its long-distance physics is still that of the weakly coupled theory. Its induced action is nonlocal and is neither weakly nor strongly coupled in any controllable sense. So a VOL14 program of controlling the induced straight-link action under repeated blocking will meet couplings that are neither small nor local after a few steps. This is the reason Bałaban's RG uses **averaged**, gauge-fixed block variables: they keep the coupling running logarithmically and the effective action quasi-local (Bałaban, *CMP* 109 (1987); 116, 119 (1988); 122 (1989)).

*Caveat:* this is leading-order perturbation theory, exploratory and in floating point. It is not a certificate. At β = 9⁵ the higher orders are negligible, and the perimeter law itself is textbook. The script is in the session workspace (`decimation_flow.py`) and can be turned into an interval-arithmetic note if useful.

## 3. Recommendation for VOL14

1. **Choose the blocking map by its coupling flow, not by RP.** Make the first VOL14 deliverable a comparison of the induced coupling flow for candidate maps: straight-link, path-averaged, and Bałaban-style averaged with axial gauge on blocks. Use a perturbative computation like the one above, then a certified version.
2. **If averaged blocking is chosen,** RP at aligned planes has to be re-examined. Averaging neighbourhoods of links in a reflection plane usually straddle that plane.
3. **Write the multiscale plan** before more single-scale certificates. Name which Bałaban estimates (small-field effective action, large-field suppression per scale, cluster expansion of nonlocal parts) are to be reproduced, and where the nonperturbative confinement-scale input enters. VOL12–VOL13 supply the large-field piece at scale 0 only.

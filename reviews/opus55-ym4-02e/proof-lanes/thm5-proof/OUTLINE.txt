OWN OUTLINE, written before reading the note's proof (from the task text only).
1. Gibbs measure on a time-periodic box Z/n x (spatial slice). Sites = links (gauge fixed: temporal gauge or not).
2. Influence matrix C(l,l') = sup over boundary pairs differing at l' of TV(cond law at l). Summed by rows or columns: alpha<1.
3. Dobrushin comparison: for two measures, or one measure with a perturbed boundary, the vector of site TV distances
   satisfies w <= b + C^T w, so w <= (I-C^T)^-1 b = sum_k (C^T)^k b.
4. Covariance of F (on slice s) with G (on slice s+t): write cov as an integral over G's value of TV between conditional and
   marginal, or use the tilted measure dmu_h = (h/mean h) dmu. That needs bounded h/Omega: a tilted measure at slice 0.
   Tilt changes conditional laws only at the links coupled to slice 0, so b supported at distance 0.
5. Project C onto the slice index: slice-to-slice kernel a(s,s') = max sum over l' in s' of C(l,l'). Random walk on Z/n with
   step weights: a(0) stay, a(1) adjacent. Green's function decays as r^|t| with r the smaller root of a1 r^2 -(1-a0) r + a1 = 0.
6. Spectral side: cov(F_0,G_t) on n-torus = sum_k c_k (lam_k/lam_0)^t (lam_k/lam_0)^(n-t) terms; take n->inf first at fixed t,
   then (lam_1/lam_0)^t <F,v1><v1,G> <= bound; then t -> inf gives lam_1/lam_0 <= r, with v1 overlap nonzero needed for a test function.
